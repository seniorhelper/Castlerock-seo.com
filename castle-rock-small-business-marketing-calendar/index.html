A Castle Rock small business marketing plan works best when it follows the town's own calendar: a reset in January, spring home projects and storm season, busy summer weekends, back-to-school in August, and the stretch from Starlighting in November through the holidays. Keep the year-round basics steady (Google Business Profile, reviews and a fast website), then launch each timed campaign four to six weeks before the demand it is aimed at.

Plenty of local businesses market in bursts: a promotion when things get slow, silence when they get busy. The problem is that search and ads take time to warm up. A campaign started the week demand peaks usually misses most of it. A simple calendar fixes that, and Castle Rock gives you plenty of fixed points to plan around.

Why plan Castle Rock marketing around a calendar?

Castle Rock is big enough to support almost any kind of local business and still growing. The Census Bureau estimates 83,815 residents as of July 2025, a 14.6% increase since 2020, across 28,219 households with a median household income of $145,197. New residents arrive every month and have to find a dentist, a plumber, a gym and a favorite restaurant from scratch, usually by searching.

The town also pulls in people who do not live here. It is the Douglas County seat, I-25 runs straight through it, and the Outlets at Castle Rock pull in interstate shoppers heading both north and south. That mix of new residents, commuters and visitors means demand moves with the season, school schedules and community events. The Castle Rock by the numbers post digs further into the Census data, and the market size calculator turns it into a customer estimate for your own service.

What should Castle Rock businesses focus on in January and February?

The holidays do not end on the same day for everyone in Castle Rock. The star on the Rock, kept up by a crew of Castle Rock firefighters known as the Keepers of the Star, stays lit through the National Western Stock Show in Denver, so the season fades out gradually. Use these quieter weeks for the work that pays off all year.

  • Ask December customers for reviews while the experience is fresh.
  • Refresh your Google Business Profile: new photos, current services, hours for the year ahead.
  • Set the annual budget and decide which two or three seasonal pushes deserve the most money.
  • Fix the website: speed, mobile layout, outdated offers and broken forms.
  • Plan content answering the questions customers asked you most last year.

Fitness, tax preparation, financial planning, home organization and wellness businesses see New Year demand, so their campaigns should already be live by the first week of January.

How should you market from March through May?

Spring is when Castle Rock homeowners start projects. Landscapers, irrigation companies, painters, fence and deck builders, cleaners and remodelers should have spring pages and ads running by March. Colorado's typical hail season begins in mid-April and runs to mid-August, so roofers, auto glass shops, body shops and restoration companies need storm-response pages and fast call handling in place before the first big storm, not after it.

Spring is also graduation and event season for families. Photographers, caterers, florists, event venues and restaurants that take large parties should publish their availability and booking details early, because families start planning weeks in advance.

What works from June through August?

Summer brings longer days, more people outdoors and more visitors passing through. Restaurants, ice cream and coffee shops, outdoor outfitters and family entertainment businesses benefit most from an accurate Google Maps presence now. People search “open now” and “near me” on their phones and pick from the first few results, so correct hours, recent photos and fresh reviews matter more than a clever ad.

By late July the focus shifts to back-to-school. Tutors, pediatric and orthodontic offices, children's activities, clothing and shoe stores, haircut salons and before-and-after-school programs should run campaigns two to four weeks before classes start. Check the Douglas County School District calendar each spring and work backward from the first day.

How should you get ready for fall?

September and October are planning months. Retailers, restaurants and gift-friendly service businesses should lock in holiday budgets now, because ad auctions get more crowded as December gets closer. Home-service companies get a second wind as owners finish projects before winter: furnace tune-ups, gutter cleaning, sprinkler blowouts, window and door replacement, and snow-removal contracts.

This is also the right time to set special holiday hours in Google Business Profile. Customers who drive to a closed store tend to remember it.

How can a business make the most of Starlighting and the holidays?

Starlighting is the Castle Rock event most locals know. The tradition of a star on the Rock dates back to 1936. In recent years the festival has run on a Saturday afternoon in late November, with the star lit in the early evening followed by fireworks. Check the Town and Chamber event pages each fall for the exact date and schedule.

  • Publish a Google Post the week of the event with holiday hours or a seasonal offer.
  • Downtown businesses near Wilcox and Perry streets can plan extended hours around festival foot traffic.
  • Share local, not generic, content: a photo of your team at the event says more than a stock holiday graphic.
  • Start gift card and holiday booking campaigns the same week.

The Castle Rock Chamber of Commerce, founded in 1955 and based downtown, is a good place to see the community calendar and meet other owners planning around the same dates.

What should stay switched on all year?

A calendar only works on top of a solid base. These are the always-on pieces that make every seasonal campaign cheaper and more effective:

A complete, accurate Google Business Profile. Categories, services, photos, hours and holiday hours. Our Google Business Profile guide has a full checklist.
A steady stream of genuine reviews. A few every month beats a burst once a year.
A fast, mobile-first website with one page for each main service and click-to-call on every page.
Consistent listings on Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and the directories that matter in your industry. The citation checklist lists them in order.
Measurement. Track calls and form leads by source so you can see which seasonal pushes paid off and which to cut next year.

FAQ

Castle Rock marketing calendar questions

When should a Castle Rock business start a seasonal campaign?

Four to six weeks before the demand it is aimed at. Search ads need time to gather data, and SEO changes can take weeks or months to show up, so starting at the peak usually means missing most of it.

What are the busiest marketing seasons in Castle Rock?

It depends on your industry. Home services peak in spring and summer, with roofing tied to hail season from mid-April to mid-August. Retail and restaurants peak around the holidays, starting with Starlighting in November. Family services peak before the school year starts.

When is Starlighting in Castle Rock?

In recent years it has been held on a Saturday in late November, with the star lit in the early evening and fireworks afterward. Check the Town and Chamber event pages each fall for the exact date.

How long does the Castle Rock star stay lit?

The star stays lit through the National Western Stock Show in Denver, which runs in January, so the holiday season in Castle Rock lasts a little longer than in many towns.

What marketing should run all year in Castle Rock?

An accurate Google Business Profile, a steady flow of genuine reviews, a fast mobile website, consistent directory listings and call and lead tracking. Seasonal campaigns work better and cost less on top of that base.

Does a small business in Castle Rock need paid ads every month?

Not always. Many businesses run ads only before and during their peak seasons and rely on local SEO and their Google Business Profile the rest of the year. The right mix depends on your margins and how quickly you need calls.
