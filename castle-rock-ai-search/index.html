AI search optimization for a Castle Rock business means making it easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand, trust and recommend the business. It relies on clear, specific answers on key pages, structured data, consistent business details across the web, genuine reviews, and making sure AI crawlers are not blocked.

About a third of U.S. adults have used ChatGPT, according to Pew Research Center, and the share keeps climbing. When someone in Castle Rock asks "who is a good kids' dentist near me," somebody gets named. It does not have to be the biggest practice. It has to be the clearest one.

Five moves that make AI recommend you

Answer first

Open each service page with one or two plain sentences: what it is, roughly what it costs, how long it takes.

Structured data

LocalBusiness, Service and FAQ schema that match what is on the page.

Consistent details

Same name, address, phone and services everywhere AI checks.

Reputation

Genuine reviews and mentions on trusted local sites.

Open the door

Do not block search-focused AI crawlers in robots.txt by accident.

Castle Rock questions, answered

Can a Castle Rock business show up in ChatGPT answers?

Yes. Clear, specific pages, structured data, consistent listings and genuine reviews all improve the chance of being recommended or cited.

What is the difference between AIO and GEO?

Both describe optimizing for AI-generated answers. AIO usually means making content easy for AI features to use; GEO usually means earning citations inside generative AI responses.

What is agentic search?

AI agents that research, compare and take actions such as filling forms or booking appointments on a user's behalf.
